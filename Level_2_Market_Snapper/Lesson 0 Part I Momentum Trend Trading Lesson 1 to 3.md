## 1.1 Introduction to Stock Trading Level 2

Welcome to the Stock Trading Level 2 Course, presented by Market Snapper. I am Adam COO, your instructor for this advanced curriculum. This course is designed to expand upon the foundational concepts established in Level 1 and introduce sophisticated methodologies for navigating the markets.

The entire course is divided into three distinct parts, each focusing on a specific trading philosophy and timeframe:

1.  **Part 1: Momentum Trend Trading**
2.  **Part 2: Counter Trend Trading**
3.  **Part 3: Intraday Trading (Day Trading)**

## 1.2 Course Structure and Methodologies

### Part 1: Momentum Trend Trading
We begin this course with Part 1, which focuses on **Momentum Trend Trading**. This approach is defined as trading *with* the trend. It serves as an advanced continuation of the trend-following concepts you learned in Level 1.

The core objective of this approach is straightforward:
*   **Buy** during a clear uptrend.
*   **Sell** (or short) during a clear downtrend.

Within Part 1, you will learn three distinct swing trading strategies. Each strategy adheres to the momentum trend trading philosophy but utilizes different entry and selection criteria.

### Part 2: Counter Trend Trading
Part 2 focuses on a methodology fundamentally different from trend following: **Counter Trend Trading**. This approach involves trading *against* the prevailing trend.

*   **Long Positions:** Buying at the bottom of a downtrend.
*   **Short Positions:** Shorting at the top of an uptrend.

In essence, Counter Trend Trading is dedicated to catching reversals in price action.

### Part 3: Intraday Trading
Part 3 focuses on **Intraday Trading**, also known as **Day Trading**. This section covers strategies executed within a single trading day, distinct from the swing trading approaches covered in Parts 1 and 2.

## 1.3 The Three Swing Trading Strategies of Part 1

The following three strategies comprise the Momentum Trend Trading curriculum. You may choose to trade all three, or select one or two based on your preference. Every strategy presented possesses a positive statistical expectancy, ensuring profitability over a sufficient sample of trades.

### Strategy 1: The Trend Retracement System (Lesson 1)
The first strategy is the **Trend Retracement System**. This system is conceptually similar to the "Balance System" introduced in Level 1, but it incorporates advanced technical tools.

*   **Technical Indicators:** Unlike the Level 1 approach, this system utilizes **Simple Moving Averages (SMA)**, **Exponential Moving Averages (EMA)**, and specific **momentum indicators**.
*   **Core Concept:** The strategy relies on entering trades during pullbacks within an established trend.
    *   **Uptrend:** Buy during a retracement.
    *   **Downtrend:** Short during a retracement.
*   **Entry Criteria:** Entries are executed on the **support levels** of the trend.

### Strategy 2: The Breakout Trading System (Lesson 2)
The second strategy is the **Breakout Trading System**. This system is designed to capture stocks as they initiate new trends following a period of consolidation.

*   **Objective:** Identify stocks that are consolidating and building energy.
*   **Execution:** Enter positions as the stock breaks out of consolidation.
    *   **Long:** Enter when breaking out into a new uptrend.
    *   **Short:** Enter when breaking down into a new downtrend.
*   **Goal:** Catch the stock at the very beginning of its new trend movement.

### Strategy 3: The MVR System (Lesson 3)
The third strategy is the **MVR System**, which stands for the **Momentum Value Rotation System**. This is a unique, simple, and highly effective strategy.

*   **Stock Selection:** Every single week, a watchlist is built consisting of **12 high-momentum, undervalued stocks**.
*   **Rotation Method:** The trader rotates among these 12 stocks to enter and exit positions.
*   **Trend Capture:** The goal is to catch these specific stocks while they are trending.
*   **Targeting:** This strategy operates with **no fixed target price**.

## 1.4 Strategy Selection and Statistical Expectancy

*   You may choose to trade two of the strategies.

Regardless of your selection, it is critical to understand that every strategy taught in this course has a **positive statistical expectancy**. When applied correctly over a sample of trades, these systems are designed to be profitable.
